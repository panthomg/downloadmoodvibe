Install MoodVibe in 4 easy ways

MoodVibe comes straight from the developer, not the Play Store. That means Android may show a few safety messages. This is normal. Pick the option that matches your phone.

⚠️ Seeing “Unsafe app blocked” or “App scan recommended”?

Android shows this for every app outside the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway. If you can’t continue, open Google Play Store settings and pause Scan apps with Play Protect. Turn it back on afterwards.

📲 Quick install

The best choice for most people and for older Android phones.

⬇️ Download & install

  1. ⬇️ Download

    Tap the button above to save moodvibe_v1.apk to your phone.

  2. 📁 Find the file

    Open your Files app or Downloads folder and tap moodvibe_v1.apk.

  3. ✅ Allow it

    If asked, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source.

  4. 🎉 Install and open

    Tap Install, then Open. Enjoy MoodVibe!

🛡️ Permission method

Use this if your phone blocks the install with an “Unverified developer” message. It works on Android 14 and newer, including in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand.

😴 One small wait. Android asks you to wait 24 hours once, as a safety step. Use your phone normally in the meantime.

  1. 🔧 Open the hidden menu

    Go to Settings › About phone and tap Build number 7 times. You’ll see “You are now a developer.”

  2. 👍 Give permission

    Go to Settings › System › Developer options and switch on Allow apps from unverified developers.

  3. 🔄 Confirm and restart

    Read the message, confirm, then restart your phone.

  4. ⏳ Wait 24 hours

    Keep the phone on and connected to the internet.

  5. 🔐 Finish setup

    Go back to Developer options, tap the setting, check it’s you with your PIN or fingerprint, and choose Indefinitely or For 7 days.

  6. 🎉 Install

    Open moodvibe_v1.apk like in Quick install.

💻 Install with a computer

Skip the 24-hour wait with a Windows PC or Mac. It uses ADB (Android Debug Bridge), an official Google tool that lets your computer talk to your phone. Apps installed this way aren’t stopped by the “Unverified developer” block.

  1. 🧰 Step 1: Get the tools on your computer

    • Open this page: Google Android Platform Tools.
    • Scroll to Downloads and click the link for your system, such as Download SDK Platform-Tools for Windows.
    • When it finishes, right-click the .zip file and choose Extract All… (on Mac, double-click it).
    • Open the new folder called platform-tools and keep it open. You’ll need it soon.

  2. 📱 Step 2: Get your phone ready

    • Go to Settings › About phone and tap Build number 7 times until it says “You are now a developer!”
    • Go back to Settings › System › Developer options and switch on USB debugging.

  3. 🔌 Step 3: Connect and say yes

    • Plug your phone into the computer with a USB cable.
    • Swipe down from the top of your phone, tap the USB notification, and pick File Transfer (also called MTP).
    • A message asks “Allow USB debugging?” Tick Always allow from this computer and tap Allow.
    • No message? Unplug the cable and plug it in again.

  4. ⌨️ Step 4: Open the command window

    • Windows: in the platform-tools folder, click the address bar at the very top (it shows something like C:\Users\…\platform-tools), type cmd and press Enter. A black window opens in the right place.
    • Mac: open Terminal, type cd (with a space after it), drag the platform-tools folder into the Terminal window, then press Enter.

  5. 🚀 Step 5: Install MoodVibe

    • Drag moodvibe_v1.apk (the file you downloaded) into the platform-tools folder.
    • In the command window, type this and press Enter (Mac users type ./adb install moodvibe_v1.apk):
    adb install moodvibe_v1.apk
    • Wait for the progress bar. When you see Success, MoodVibe is on your phone. 🎉
👍 All done? After you see “Success”, you can safely unplug your phone.

📱 Phone only, no computer

No wait and no cable. Shizuku is a free app that lets your phone install apps for itself, using Android’s Wireless Debugging.

  1. 🏪 Get Shizuku

    Download Shizuku from the Google Play Store.

  2. 📶 Turn on Wireless debugging

    Go to Settings › System › Developer options and switch on Wireless debugging.

  3. 🔗 Pair it

    Open Shizuku and follow its on-screen steps to pair using the code.

  4. 📦 Get an installer

    From the Play Store, download SAI (Split APKs Installer) or App Manager.

  5. 🎉 Install MoodVibe

    Open that app, pick moodvibe_v1.apk and install it.

Questions? We’ve got answers

It says “App not installed”. What now?

You may have an older MoodVibe with a different signature. Uninstall the old one, then install the new file again.

Why is the file 127 MB?

This early build is a “Debug” version with extra files that help us fix bugs. Future Release versions will be much smaller, around 5–10 MB.

Is my data safe if I skip the Play Protect warning?

Yes. Choosing “Install anyway” only tells Android you trust this one file. It doesn’t open your phone to other apps. MoodVibe is local-first, so your mood logs are kept on your phone and in your private Firebase account.
